The Amazing Pangolin

The pangolin is one of the most unique and fascinating animals in the world. It is sometimes called a "scaly anteater" because of its diet and appearance. Pangolins are the only mammals completely covered in scales, which protect them from predators. When threatened, they curl into a tight ball, making it very difficult for predators to harm them. I like pangolins because they are unusual, peaceful creatures that play an important role in nature by controlling insect populations. Sadly, they are also one of the most trafficked mammals in the world, which makes learning about them even more important.

Interesting Facts About Pangolins

  • Pangolins eat ants and termites using their long, sticky tongues.
  • They have strong claws for digging into insect nests.
  • There are eight different species of pangolins.
  • Their scales are made of keratin, the same material as human nails.
  • They are mostly nocturnal animals.
